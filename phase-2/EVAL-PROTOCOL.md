# Brand Expert Core — Evaluation Protocol

Status: PHASE 2 EVALUATION SPECIFICATION

Purpose: test whether the operational Brand Expert Core can diagnose unfamiliar identity systems without relying on agency reputation, case-study rationale, famous-case memory, or stylistic preference.

## 1. Non-negotiable separation

The blind evaluator must **not** have access to `BLIND-HOLDOUTS.md`, agency names, case-study text, strategic rationale, awards, editorial reviews, or truth labels before producing its diagnosis.

The process needs two roles:

### EVALUATOR
Receives anonymized visual evidence + the Phase 2 operating documents only.

### SCORER / TRUTH KEEPER
Holds the holdout key and source truth, reveals it only after the evaluator's answer is frozen, and scores the response.

Do not use one contaminated conversation/session for both roles.

## 2. What the evaluator receives

For each case provide a compact but system-revealing visual package where available:

- primary mark / wordmark;
- 4–8 representative applications;
- typography examples;
- color relationships;
- graphic-language examples;
- motion frames/clip for motion-led cases;
- digital/product or physical examples where central.

Remove or conceal:

- agency name;
- awards;
- explanatory captions/rationale;
- editorial commentary;
- project title if it reveals the strategic story beyond unavoidable brand naming.

Do not intentionally remove brand assets that are part of the design merely to make the task artificial.

## 3. Evaluator prompt/output

The evaluator must return, before any reveal:

### A. OBSERVATION
What is directly visible:
- components;
- constants;
- variables;
- hierarchy;
- repeated devices;
- static/motion behavior;
- apparent application range.

### B. SYSTEM LOGIC
Best-supported explanation of how the pieces relate and vary.

### C. APPARENT DESIGN PROBLEM
What problem the system appears optimized to solve. Must be labeled inference.

### D. CREATIVE JUDGMENT
Strengths and weaknesses across specificity, coherence, range, operationality, craft, generative power.

### E. RISKS / FAILURE MODES
Genericity, literalization, category camouflage, one-trick behavior, no fallback, cultural assumptions, etc.

### F. UNKNOWN
What cannot be concluded from visuals.

The evaluator is explicitly forbidden to infer business effectiveness from appearance.

## 4. Freeze-before-reveal rule

The evaluator answer receives a timestamp/hash or immutable saved record before the truth is revealed.

No editing after reveal.

Any post-reveal commentary must be a separate `CALIBRATION NOTE` and cannot alter the original score.

## 5. Truth reveal

After freezing the answer, reveal only what is required to score:

- project/brand;
- agency/client source;
- source-stated problem;
- source-stated intent;
- documented system mechanics;
- known evidence boundaries.

Independent editorial reception may be revealed after source truth if needed for calibration, but must not become the correctness oracle for subjective judgments.

## 6. Scoring rubric — 100 points

### 1. Observation accuracy — 25
Correctly identifies visible components, relationships, invariants and variables without invention.

### 2. System-logic diagnosis — 20
Explains how the design operates rather than merely describing its look.

### 3. Strategy/design inference discipline — 15
Makes plausible bounded inferences and clearly labels them as inference; avoids invented intent.

### 4. Creative judgment quality — 15
Identifies material strengths/trade-offs with mechanism-level reasoning rather than taste.

### 5. Distinctiveness/evidence discipline — 10
Does not falsely call observed devices measured distinctive assets or infer market recognition without evidence.

### 6. Failure-mode detection — 10
Recognizes genericity, literalization, scalability, governance, motion/technical or cultural risks where present.

### 7. Unknown handling — 5
Correctly identifies what visuals cannot establish.

## 7. Penalties

Apply in addition to rubric deductions:

- invented factual/source claim: **-20 each**;
- unsupported business-effectiveness claim: **-15 each**;
- false distinctive-asset measurement claim: **-10 each**;
- agency/award/reputation knowledge used before reveal: **case invalid / rerun with replacement**;
- contamination from holdout truth: **case invalid**.

## 8. Pass thresholds

### CASE PASS
75/100 minimum, with:
- no CRITICAL failure from `FAILURE-MODES.md`;
- at least 15/25 Observation;
- at least 12/20 System Logic.

### PHASE 2 PASS
Minimum:
- 6 valid blind cases completed;
- mean score ≥ 80;
- no more than one case below 75;
- zero critical evidence-integrity failures;
- at least one valid case each covering physical/packaging, motion/digital, and corporate/product system behavior.

These thresholds are project calibration criteria, not externally validated psychometric standards.

## 9. Calibration after each case

For each miss write:

`MISS → FAILURE MODE → WHY IT HAPPENED → OPERATING RULE TO ADJUST → REGRESSION RISK`

Do not rewrite rules merely because the evaluator disagrees with an agency's aesthetic choice. Calibrate only when the miss reveals a reasoning/evidence failure or a repeatable weakness.

## 10. Regression set

After calibration, rerun previously passed **non-blind calibration material**, not burned blind cases, to ensure fixes did not create new problems such as:

- excessive caution;
- refusal to make creative judgments;
- over-penalizing expressiveness;
- preference for minimalism;
- rejecting metaphor categorically;
- treating every category convention as generic.

## 11. Release gate for real client work

The Expert Core may be used as a trusted input to consequential visual-brand definition only after:

1. Phase 2 operating documents are internally consistent;
2. blind evaluation passes the Phase 2 threshold;
3. critical misses are calibrated;
4. regression checks pass;
5. the output still distinguishes evidence, interpretation and decision.

Until then it is a candidate expert system, not validated expertise.
