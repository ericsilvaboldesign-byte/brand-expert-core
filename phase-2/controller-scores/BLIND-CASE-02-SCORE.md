## Truth comparison

The source record describes Fanta's first unified global identity, designed to work across a very large flavor portfolio and many markets. The evaluator independently recovered the core portfolio logic: a fixed brand masthead anchored by blue and a variable flavor layer, with a production-friendly kit of parts that can scale across many SKUs.

The evaluator also correctly identified playfulness as a major design objective, the role of the fixed blue anchor, the flavor-color architecture, fruit/splash illustration grammar, and packaging governance. This closely matches the documented system: brighter blue, redesigned logo, custom Fanta Pop / Fanta Sans typography, flavor-specific color systems, custom illustrations, and packaging designed to operate across a global flavor range.

The evaluator appropriately withheld before/after equity claims because predecessor material was not supplied, even though the real project explicitly evolved previous Fanta assets rather than treating the brand as a blank slate.

## Material misses / calibration notes

1. `playful/youthful fun` is slightly narrower than the documented intention, which emphasized playfulness for people of all ages. Minor inference overreach, not a factual integrity failure.
2. The evaluator introduced general category context about playful FMCG redesign trends. It was labeled as external professional knowledge, so no penalty, but future blind runs should keep category priors explicitly subordinate to package evidence.
3. The supplied package did not expose the full typography, photography, mixed-media, or motion system, so the evaluator could not recover those dimensions; it correctly placed them under UNKNOWN rather than inventing them.
