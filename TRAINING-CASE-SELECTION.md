# Core Training Case Selection — Phase 1C

Status: SELECTED FOR ACQUISITION / OBSERVATION — NOT YET VERIFIED TRAINING CASES

Purpose: freeze the first serious brand-transformation training set. Selection is based on learning value, evidence availability, and corpus diversity — not fame, awards, or aesthetic preference.

## Selection gates

A case enters this set only if it offers:

1. a real brand/business problem rather than a cosmetic logo exercise;
2. a traceable chain from source-stated strategic intent to an observable identity system;
3. enough primary material to study system logic rather than isolated hero images;
4. a meaningful before/after or inherited-equity question where relevant;
5. a learning contribution not already overrepresented in the corpus;
6. no conflict with reserved blind-holdout or known-canon calibration cases.

Editorial sources such as Brand New, Brandemia, BP&O, Design Week, Identity Designed, DIELINE and The Brand Identity are used for discovery and critique. Agency/client material remains primary for what the project team says it intended and produced.

## The 12 selected cases

### 1. Slack — Pentagram

**Learning role:** equity preservation + simplification + B2B/product brand coherence.

Why selected:
- inherited a recognizable but reproduction-problematic octothorpe;
- explicit decision to preserve brand equity rather than replace the core symbol;
- clear reduction of an over-complex color system;
- symbol components become a broader graphic language;
- strong bridge between brand and software/product context.

Primary source: https://www.pentagram.com/work/slack

Key question: **How do you simplify aggressively without erasing accumulated recognition?**

### 2. PayPal — Pentagram

**Learning role:** global fintech refresh + product behavior translated into motion.

Why selected:
- explicit business evolution toward a more universal offering;
- strategic principles are stated before the visual solution;
- custom type, palette, photography and motion are documented as one system;
- motion behavior derives from familiar payment gestures such as tapping, flipping and swiping;
- demonstrates how a mature digital brand can separate symbol and wordmark for greater flexibility.

Primary source: https://www.pentagram.com/work/paypal

Key question: **How can product behavior become brand behavior without becoming a gimmick?**

### 3. The John Lewis Partnership — Pentagram

**Learning role:** brand architecture + heritage assets + retail/packaging system.

Why selected:
- three related identities must work separately and together;
- employee ownership is elevated as a strategic differentiator;
- historical pattern equity is reinterpreted rather than discarded;
- typography, color, pattern, packaging, retail and verbal/naming decisions coexist;
- multiple levels of expression are designed for a very large product/service portfolio.

Primary source: https://www.pentagram.com/work/the-john-lewis-partnership

Key question: **How do you create coherence across a brand family without flattening the individual brands?**

### 4. GSK — Wolff Olins

**Learning role:** corporate transformation + science-led identity + multi-environment expression.

Why selected:
- business restructuring/de-merger created a genuine strategic inflection point;
- scope includes strategy, architecture, visual, verbal, employer brand, environments and digital experience;
- bioscientific imagery is central to the expression rather than generic technology imagery;
- useful case for examining first-party outcome claims separately from observed design quality.

Primary source: https://www.wolffolins.com/work/gsk

Key question: **How does a large corporate brand turn a strategic business change into a coherent identity across audiences and environments?**

### 5. Wise — Ragged Edge

**Learning role:** category challenger + global brand + retained symbol equity.

Why selected:
- strategy reframes the company beyond “better bank” toward a broader money-system ambition;
- explicitly retains equity in the fast-flag symbol while redrawing it for scalability;
- verbal, typographic, color and graphic tapestry systems are tied to a global idea;
- useful counterpoint to conservative finance branding.

Primary source: https://raggededge.com/partnerships/wise

Key question: **How can a global financial brand become more expressive without losing clarity or trust?**

### 6. Burger King — JKR

**Learning role:** heritage revival + QSR + packaging/physical brand + distinctive assets.

Why selected:
- business transformation preceded the identity refresh;
- the redesign deliberately rejects a more synthetic 2000s visual era and returns to stronger historical brand codes;
- packaging, color, typography, food photography and restaurant expression can be studied together;
- provides a strong case for asset preservation/revival versus novelty.

Primary source: https://www.jkrglobal.com/work/burger-king

Key question: **When is looking backward strategically stronger than inventing a completely new visual language?**

Boundary: agency-reported commercial metrics must be treated as first-party claims until independently verified.

### 7. Reddit — Pentagram

**Learning role:** community platform + mascot governance + motion + system reduction.

Why selected:
- the problem is explicit brand-material proliferation and inconsistency;
- preserves Snoo and community vernacular instead of sanitizing them;
- turns the conversation bubble into a repeatable system across typography, composition and motion;
- reduces a palette of 100+ colors while preserving community customization;
- useful governance case for balancing central coherence with decentralized expression.

Primary source: https://www.pentagram.com/work/reddit/story

Key question: **How do you systematize a participatory brand without destroying the culture that made it distinctive?**

### 8. Banco Itaú — Pentagram + Itaú in-house

**Learning role:** Latin American heritage + financial brand + distinctive-shape evolution.

Why selected:
- one of Brazil’s most established financial brands at its centenary;
- preserves and evolves a familiar rounded-square asset into the proprietary `pedra` rather than starting over;
- moves orange from supporting color to primary brand color;
- creates a graphic language from the evolved shape;
- strong non-Anglo-American case with explicit heritage/future tension.

Primary source: https://www.pentagram.com/work/banco-itau

Key question: **How far can an established distinctive asset evolve before it stops carrying its accumulated meaning?**

### 9. San Francisco Symphony — COLLINS

**Learning role:** cultural repositioning + dynamic typography + computational/motion identity.

Why selected:
- tackles a category-level relevance problem rather than a cosmetic refresh;
- organizational and artistic change is part of the context;
- responsive/variable typography reacts to sound and music;
- combines traditional typographic cues with algorithmic behavior;
- connects directly to the Expert Core’s dynamic/generative and creative-technology layer.

Primary source: https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/san-francisco-symphony/

Key question: **How can computational behavior express the subject of a brand while remaining a repeatable identity system?**

Boundary: impact metrics and causal language on the agency page remain first-party claims until independently verified.

### 10. Samyang — Pentagram

**Learning role:** Asian corporate/consumer transformation + naming/architecture + packaging bridge.

Why selected:
- Korean company expanding from familiar noodle manufacturer toward a broader global food/technology/sustainability ambition;
- new corporate name and strategy address multiple business areas;
- circle/square contradiction is explicitly connected to strategy and naming;
- includes guidelines and packaging/application templates;
- improves geographic and cultural diversity of the corpus.

Primary source: https://www.pentagram.com/work/samyang

Key question: **When does a simple geometric idea genuinely organize a system, and when would it merely literalize strategy?**

### 11. Sharjah City Brand — Pentagram / Right to Left

**Learning role:** place branding + bilingual identity + motion + cross-script parity.

Why selected:
- adds civic/place branding rather than another corporation;
- strategy connects history, culture, tourism, industry and future ambition;
- English and Arabic are designed simultaneously rather than as primary/secondary translations;
- shared arch device operates across language, motion, iconography and imagery;
- provides a valuable test of cultural and linguistic design assumptions.

Primary source: https://www.pentagram.com/work/sharjah-city-brand

Key question: **How can one identity behave fluently across two scripts without forcing one language to imitate the other?**

### 12. Melissa — Pentagram

**Learning role:** Brazilian fashion + expressive identity + architecture + art-direction range.

Why selected:
- iconic Brazilian fashion brand with strong cultural and collaboration history;
- explicit tension between retaining spirit/heritage and broadening beyond a teenage audience;
- `kaleidoscope` idea becomes a framing system for patterns, textures and imagery rather than only a logo treatment;
- includes brand architecture restructuring and later custom-type expansion;
- adds fashion/art-direction behavior and another Latin American case.

Primary sources:
- https://www.pentagram.com/work/melissa/story
- https://www.pentagram.com/work/melissa-1

Key question: **How can a highly expressive system gain sophistication without becoming visually generic or losing its cultural energy?**

## Corpus coverage after selection

This set intentionally spans:
- B2B/product technology — Slack
- global payments/finance — PayPal, Wise
- retail architecture/packaging — John Lewis Partnership
- corporate health/science — GSK
- heritage consumer/QSR — Burger King
- participatory digital platform — Reddit
- Latin American financial heritage — Itaú
- cultural/computational identity — San Francisco Symphony
- Asian food/corporate transformation — Samyang
- civic/place + bilingual identity — Sharjah
- fashion/art direction — Melissa

It also creates different strategic tensions:
- preserve vs replace;
- simplify vs retain richness;
- parent coherence vs sub-brand autonomy;
- heritage vs future;
- global consistency vs local/cultural fluency;
- static recognition vs dynamic expression;
- central governance vs community expression;
- restrained vs expressive systems.

## Explicit exclusions from training

Do not use the reserved blind-holdout cases as training: Perplexity, Audible, Papier, Fanta, Decathlon, Instagram motion system, Bolt, PAC NYC, or the recorded backup Utah Jazz.

Do not use known-canon calibration cases as training: Airbnb, Mailchimp, The Met, FedEx, McDonald’s, NASA 1975 graphics standards.

## Promotion rule

Selection does **not** make a case a VERIFIED TRAINING CASE.

For each selected case, next build an evidence dossier:

BEFORE STATE → SOURCE-STATED PROBLEM → SOURCE-STATED STRATEGY → PRIMARY VISUAL EVIDENCE → OBSERVED SYSTEM → PRESERVED / CHANGED → EDITORIAL CRITIQUE → LATER EVIDENCE → LIMITATIONS

A case is promoted only after enough primary visual material has been directly inspected to analyze system logic under `CASE-EVIDENCE-STANDARD.md`.
