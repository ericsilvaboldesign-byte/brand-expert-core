# Open-Access Branding Sources

Status: SOURCE ACQUISITION PLAN — NOT YET AUTHORITATIVE KNOWLEDGE

Purpose: identify legally accessible full-text branding/identity resources that can strengthen the Brand Expert Core without confusing free availability with authority.

## Inclusion rule

A resource is not promoted merely because it is free, downloadable, popular or described as a 'branding book'.

For each candidate distinguish:
- authority / provenance
- actual content access
- construct(s) covered
- professional vs empirical status
- limitations

## PRIORITY A — strong open-access additions

### Paul Capriotti — Branding corporativo / Gestión estratégica de la identidad corporativa

Why prioritize:
- established academic/professional author in corporate identity / corporate branding
- an open-access 2010 article in *Comunicación* provides directly accessible text on managing corporate identity, image/reputation, identity-profile analysis, and communication
- the 2009 book *Branding corporativo: fundamentos para la gestión estratégica de la identidad corporativa* is bibliographically established; only treat the full book as content-verified if a legitimate full-text copy can be accessed

Use for:
- corporate identity
- identity vs image/reputation
- strategic identity management
- publics/stakeholders

Boundary:
Do not treat Capriotti as empirical proof for every branding construct. Keep his model distinct from Keller, Aaker, Kapferer, Romaniuk and other constructs.

### WIPO / OMPI — El secreto está en la marca

Why prioritize:
- official World Intellectual Property Organization publication
- legally open-access
- directly downloadable in Spanish and other languages
- strong authority for trademark / legal-commercial use of marks

Use for:
- trademarks as business assets
- types of marks
- creation/protection/use/commercialization of marks
- legal / IP boundary of branding

Boundary:
This is primarily an intellectual-property/business guide, not a visual-identity methodology or brand-strategy canon.

### Foundations in Digital Marketing — Rochelle Grayson / BCcampus

Status:
OPEN TEXTBOOK / ACADEMIC-PROFESSIONAL EDUCATION.

Access:
Legitimately open. The Open Textbook Library lists online, PDF, eBook and XML formats under CC BY. The book is intended as an introductory digital-marketing textbook and includes visual storytelling, design principles/frameworks, websites/landing pages, content, social, mobile, email, attribution and analytics.

Use for:
- digital marketing context around brand execution
- visual storytelling in digital channels
- channel behavior and measurement context
- website/content/social/mobile execution vocabulary

Boundary:
This is not a canonical brand-identity or visual-identity methodology. Do not let digital-marketing channel logic govern core brand strategy or identity design. Treat its design material as educational/professional context unless a specific claim is independently supported.

## PRIORITY B — useful professional-practice material

### Daniel Rowles — Digital Branding

Verified bibliographic status:
Kogan Page publishes the 4th edition, *Digital Branding: How to Successfully Build and Measure a Brand Online*, published 3 January 2025. The publisher describes coverage of brand strategy, identity development, digital channels, analytics, SEO, social algorithms and generative AI, and offers a sample chapter.

Use as:
PRIMARY PROFESSIONAL / PRACTITIONER BOOK where actual content is legally accessed.

Useful for:
- digital brand planning
- digital presence and measurement
- relationship between identity and digital channels
- contemporary practitioner treatment of analytics, SEO, social and GenAI

Critical access boundary:
Do NOT ingest the linked university-hosted file merely because it is reachable. Its filename identifies a PDFDrive copy. Unless the repository explicitly establishes authorization/licensing for that full-text copy, treat it as an access lead, not an approved corpus source. Prefer the publisher sample, licensed library access, author material, or another clearly lawful source.

Do not describe this as 'the bible' of digital branding; that is promotional language, not an evidence classification.

### Joe Kutchera, Hilda García, Alonso Fernández — E-X-I-T-O

Verified bibliographic status:
Google Books records *E-X-I-T-O: Su Estrategia de Marketing Digital en 5 Pasos*, Grupo Editorial Patria, 2014, 256 pages.

Use as:
PROFESSIONAL / REGIONAL DIGITAL-MARKETING PRACTICE.

Useful for:
- Latin American / Hispanic digital-marketing context
- customer interaction, social channels and campaign planning
- regional examples and terminology

Boundary:
This is primarily a digital-marketing framework, not a visual-identity or brand-strategy authority. Do not generalize its five-step structure into Brand Expert Core methodology unless later synthesis independently justifies a comparable construct.

### DigitalMarketer — The Ultimate Guide to Digital Marketing

Status:
FIRST-PARTY PRACTITIONER / CONTENT-MARKETING GUIDE.

The official DigitalMarketer page presents it as its own operational guide to digital strategy, content, social, search, paid media, email, metrics and roles.

Use for:
- contemporary practitioner vocabulary
- channel orchestration
- execution/measurement context

Boundary:
It is not independent research and not a brand-identity source. Claims about effectiveness are self-published practitioner claims unless independently verified. Keep outside the core identity/visual methodology layer.

### Summa Branding — identity / digital-branding resources

Use as:
PRIMARY PROFESSIONAL / AGENCY PRACTICE where content is published by Summa about its own methods or projects.

Useful for:
- identity-system practice
- digital branding
- Spanish/Ibero-American professional context
- case discovery

Boundary:
Agency frameworks and claims are professional practice, not empirical law.

### Brandemia reading guides and professional resources

Use as:
EDITORIAL / PROFESSIONAL DISCOVERY.

Useful for:
- identifying books and authors
- mapping professional discourse
- discovering visual-identity and strategy references
- finding Spanish-language/Ibero-American practitioners and cases

Boundary:
Do not treat Brandemia's summaries of books as verification of what those books establish. Trace important claims to the original work.

## PRIORITY C — introductory / orientation only

### Doppler — Branding: paso a paso / marcas inolvidables

Use only as:
INTRODUCTORY PROFESSIONAL CONTENT.

Useful for:
- seeing common beginner-level branding vocabulary
- identifying claims that should be checked against stronger sources

Do not use as canonical methodology or expert authority.

Do not automatically accept claims about color adaptation, logo behavior, responsive identity or digital consistency unless the exact claim is present and can be supported by stronger design/identity sources.

### Communitools — branding/rebranding manual

Use only as:
INTRODUCTORY / PROFESSIONAL COMMENTARY.

The accessible article presents broad branding history, famous-brand examples, and generic rebranding recommendations. It can help map commonly repeated practitioner claims, but those claims require stronger sourcing before entering authoritative knowledge.

## AGGREGATORS — DISCOVERY ONLY

### InfoLibros

InfoLibros is useful as a discovery/index layer because it currently lists downloadable branding materials including Capriotti, WIPO, Doppler, Summa and others.

Do not cite InfoLibros as the authority when the original publisher/author source can be found.

Correct workflow:
InfoLibros → locate title → trace to original author/publisher/institution → verify legal/full-text access → ingest original source.

### Academia.edu mirrors/uploads

Use only for discovery or access when provenance and upload legitimacy are clear.

Prefer:
- author's institutional repository
- journal/publisher
- official university repository
- DOI source
- author's own site

over an Academia.edu upload.

### Dokumen / PDFDrive-derived mirrors / miscellaneous full-book mirrors

Use as discovery only unless authorization is explicit.

A technically accessible full book is not automatically a lawful or provenance-safe corpus source. Record the canonical title and trace to publisher/author/library access rather than normalizing mirror copies into the Expert Core.

## Claims from secondary AI/search summaries — DO NOT INGEST AS RULES

The following kinds of statements may be useful hypotheses but are not accepted merely because a search summary repeats them:

- 'digital brands must mutate responsively'
- 'brands are co-created every day through social conversation'
- 'omnichannel consistency means the same tone, colors and values everywhere'

These require construct-level sourcing and qualification.

In particular, identity-system consistency must not be reduced to visual sameness. A coherent identity may deliberately vary by context while maintaining identifiable constants. Any principle about responsive identity, co-creation or omnichannel behavior must be traced to a source that actually establishes it and bounded to its context.

## Do not prioritize for the Expert Core

- generic personal-branding collections
- SEO ebooks
- lead-generation ebooks with little sourcing
- 'best branding books' summaries as evidence
- anonymous PDFs
- secondary copies where an original source exists

These may be useful for mapping common professional discourse, but should not shape expert methodology.

## Key principle

FREE ACCESS solves an access problem.
It does not solve an authority problem.

DIGITAL MARKETING is adjacent to DIGITAL BRANDING, but it is not the same construct as BRAND IDENTITY or VISUAL IDENTITY.

The Expert Core should prefer a smaller number of fully readable, correctly classified sources over a large folder of unvetted free PDFs.
