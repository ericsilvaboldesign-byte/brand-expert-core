# Open-Access Branding Sources

Status: SOURCE ACQUISITION PLAN — NOT YET AUTHORITATIVE KNOWLEDGE

Purpose: identify legally accessible full-text branding/identity resources that can strengthen the Brand Expert Core without confusing free availability with authority.

## Inclusion rule

A resource is not promoted merely because it is free, downloadable, popular or described as a 'branding book'.

For each candidate distinguish:
- authority / provenance
- actual content access
- construct(s) covered
- professional vs empirical status
- limitations

## PRIORITY A — strong open-access additions

### Paul Capriotti — Branding corporativo / Gestión estratégica de la identidad corporativa

Why prioritize:
- established academic/professional author in corporate identity / corporate branding
- an open-access 2010 article in *Comunicación* provides directly accessible text on managing corporate identity, image/reputation, identity-profile analysis, and communication
- the 2009 book *Branding corporativo: fundamentos para la gestión estratégica de la identidad corporativa* is bibliographically established; only treat the full book as content-verified if a legitimate full-text copy can be accessed

Use for:
- corporate identity
- identity vs image/reputation
- strategic identity management
- publics/stakeholders

Boundary:
Do not treat Capriotti as empirical proof for every branding construct. Keep his model distinct from Keller, Aaker, Kapferer, Romaniuk and other constructs.

### WIPO / OMPI — El secreto está en la marca

Why prioritize:
- official World Intellectual Property Organization publication
- legally open-access
- directly downloadable in Spanish and other languages
- strong authority for trademark / legal-commercial use of marks

Use for:
- trademarks as business assets
- types of marks
- creation/protection/use/commercialization of marks
- legal / IP boundary of branding

Boundary:
This is primarily an intellectual-property/business guide, not a visual-identity methodology or brand-strategy canon.

## PRIORITY B — useful professional-practice material

### Summa Branding — identity / digital-branding resources

Use as:
PRIMARY PROFESSIONAL / AGENCY PRACTICE where content is published by Summa about its own methods or projects.

Useful for:
- identity-system practice
- digital branding
- Spanish/Ibero-American professional context
- case discovery

Boundary:
Agency frameworks and claims are professional practice, not empirical law.

### Brandemia reading guides and professional resources

Use as:
EDITORIAL / PROFESSIONAL DISCOVERY.

Useful for:
- identifying books and authors
- mapping professional discourse
- discovering visual-identity and strategy references
- finding Spanish-language/Ibero-American practitioners and cases

Boundary:
Do not treat Brandemia's summaries of books as verification of what those books establish. Trace important claims to the original work.

## PRIORITY C — introductory / orientation only

### Doppler — Branding: paso a paso / marcas inolvidables

Use only as:
INTRODUCTORY PROFESSIONAL CONTENT.

Useful for:
- seeing common beginner-level branding vocabulary
- identifying claims that should be checked against stronger sources

Do not use as canonical methodology or expert authority.

### Communitools — branding/rebranding manual

Use only as:
INTRODUCTORY / PROFESSIONAL COMMENTARY.

The accessible article presents broad branding history, famous-brand examples, and generic rebranding recommendations. It can help map commonly repeated practitioner claims, but those claims require stronger sourcing before entering authoritative knowledge.

## AGGREGATORS — DISCOVERY ONLY

### InfoLibros

InfoLibros is useful as a discovery/index layer because it currently lists downloadable branding materials including Capriotti, WIPO, Doppler, Summa and others.

Do not cite InfoLibros as the authority when the original publisher/author source can be found.

Correct workflow:
InfoLibros → locate title → trace to original author/publisher/institution → verify legal/full-text access → ingest original source.

### Academia.edu mirrors/uploads

Use only for discovery or access when provenance and upload legitimacy are clear.

Prefer:
- author's institutional repository
- journal/publisher
- official university repository
- DOI source
- author's own site

over an Academia.edu upload.

## Do not prioritize for the Expert Core

- generic personal-branding collections
- SEO ebooks
- lead-generation ebooks with little sourcing
- 'best branding books' summaries as evidence
- anonymous PDFs
- secondary copies where an original source exists

These may be useful for mapping common professional discourse, but should not shape expert methodology.

## Key principle

FREE ACCESS solves an access problem.
It does not solve an authority problem.

The Expert Core should prefer a smaller number of fully readable, correctly classified sources over a large folder of unvetted free PDFs.
