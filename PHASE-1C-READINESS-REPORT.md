# Phase 1C Readiness Report

Date: 2026-08-19

Status: **READY FOR PHASE 2 — BOUNDED**

## Decision

Phase 1C core training-case work is complete enough to begin expert synthesis.

The gate is passed because all 12 selected brand-transformation cases now have source-separated dossiers and directly inspected visual evidence sufficient to analyze a bounded slice of system logic under `CASE-EVIDENCE-STANDARD.md`.

This is not a claim that the corpus contains 12 fully observed identities. It contains 12 **VERIFIED — BOUNDED** training cases and 0 FULL VISUAL PRIMARY cases.

## Completed cases

Slack, PayPal, The John Lewis Partnership, GSK, Wise, Burger King, Reddit, Banco Itaú, San Francisco Symphony, Samyang, Sharjah City Brand, Melissa.

See `TRAINING-CASE-CENSUS.md` for exact scope and limitations.

## What the case set now teaches

The set is sufficient to study recurring but non-universal professional decisions across:

- equity preservation vs replacement;
- heritage reuse vs clean break;
- brand architecture and shared-system governance;
- category conformity vs challenger expression;
- corporate transformation and purpose translation;
- packaging/physical vs digital/product expression;
- participatory/community brand governance;
- distinctive-asset evolution without falsely claiming measured distinctiveness;
- computational/dynamic typography and motion tied to subject matter;
- multilingual/cross-script parity;
- literal strategy-to-form translation and its risks;
- high-expression fashion/art-direction systems.

## Evidence boundaries that Phase 2 MUST preserve

1. `AGENCY/CLIENT-STATED` is not the same as `OBSERVED`.
2. `OBSERVED` is not the same as `EMPIRICALLY EFFECTIVE`.
3. Visual carriers reproducing primary artifacts must remain flagged when the original media CDN was inaccessible.
4. First-party outcome metrics are correlation/claim evidence unless independently validated.
5. A recurring visual feature is not automatically a measured distinctive asset.
6. A compelling metaphor is not automatically a scalable system.
7. No universal design recipe may be inferred from 12 cases.

## Holdout integrity

Blind holdouts remain untouched as training material. Known-canon calibration cases remain separate. Phase 2 synthesis must not analyze or ingest the reserved blind set before evaluation.

**OPEN.**

Phase 2 may now synthesize the verified source corpus + professional-practice layers + 12 bounded training cases into an operational Brand Expert Core. It must produce testable expert behavior, not a new pile of generic branding principles.
