# San Francisco Symphony — Phase 1C Training Case

Status: VERIFIED TRAINING CASE — BOUNDED PARTIAL VISUAL PRIMARY

Learning role: cultural repositioning + computational typography + motion-led identity.

Scope boundary: verified for the institutional-relevance problem, heritage/contemporary tension, variable/responsive typography, sound-reactive behavior, Bay Area palette and poster/digital expression. Full codebase, sonic system, every motion state and independent causal outcomes remain incomplete.

## BEFORE STATE
COLLINS frames classical music as suffering from a relevance/perception problem: old-world, elite and static. The Symphony simultaneously faced leadership and organizational change, including a new artistic model and broader experimentation.

## SOURCE-STATED PROBLEM / STRATEGY
COLLINS states that it was invited to clarify and express a new vision for the Symphony and help reassert classical music as a contemporary global art form while staying rooted in community and legacy.

Primary source: https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/san-francisco-symphony/

## OBSERVED SYSTEM
Primary case text and directly inspected visual carriers show:
- classical serif-derived forms retained as a heritage reference;
- variable/responsive letterforms stretched and distorted into extreme but recognizable states;
- typography used as the core image rather than supporting decoration;
- motion behavior tied to sound/music rather than generic transition effects;
- black/white formal base expanded with contemporary Bay Area-inspired colors;
- outdoor collaborator posters use tall expressive type with portraiture, demonstrating that the dynamic system can also resolve into static physical applications.

Visual carriers inspected:
- The One Show dynamic typography archive
- Muse by Clios physical poster coverage
- COLLINS primary case-page gallery references.

## PRESERVED / CHANGED
Preserved: classical typographic cues, institutional seriousness, music as the subject.
Changed: typography becomes algorithmically flexible, motion becomes structural, palette becomes broader and programming is expressed with more range.

## SYSTEM LOGIC
The strongest lesson is that the computation is not the idea by itself. Variable behavior is anchored to the actual property of the subject—music changing over time. A conventional heritage signifier becomes the substrate for contemporary motion rather than being replaced with generic futuristic graphics.

## CRITIQUE / LIMITS
COLLINS currently reports donor, attendance and coherence impacts. These are agency-presented outcome claims and are not treated as independent causal evidence.

Training boundary: use this case for strategy-to-behavior translation and computational identity. Do not infer that sound-reactive type is appropriate for unrelated brands merely because it is technically impressive.
