# Wise — Phase 1C Training Case

Status: VERIFIED TRAINING CASE — BOUNDED PARTIAL VISUAL PRIMARY

Learning role: challenger finance + global expressive system + retained symbol equity.

Scope boundary: verified for strategy, retained fast-flag equity, Wise Sans, green system, tapestries, product/card expression and global rollout logic. Complete photography/motion library and causal effectiveness remain incomplete.

## BEFORE STATE
Wise had grown from TransferWise into a broader money product while facing imitators and a category visually dominated by conservative financial codes.

## SOURCE-STATED PROBLEM / STRATEGY
Ragged Edge states that the rebrand was intended to help Wise stand out from imitators and move beyond money transfer toward a broader proposition: not a better bank, but a new money system for people and businesses globally. The organizing idea is `The World's Money`.

Primary source: https://raggededge.com/partnerships/wise

## OBSERVED SYSTEM
Primary/official and editorial visual carriers directly inspected show:
- fast-flag symbol retained and redrawn;
- transition from blue/navy toward a high-salience green system;
- bold, condensed/characterful custom typography;
- green and dark cards with clear Wise signature;
- global-language/currency messaging and culturally varied graphic campaigns;
- large-format billboards using currency names and short copy;
- product UI and physical card expression sharing the same core palette.

Primary current design source: https://wise.design/foundations/cards
Editorial visual carrier: Brand New review of the 2023 identity.

## PRESERVED / CHANGED
Preserved: fast-flag recognition and direct-product ethos.
Changed: category color, typography, visual breadth, global-cultural expression and the move from transfer-specific framing to a system-wide money brand.

## SYSTEM LOGIC
The identity does not rely on one decorative motif. Recognition is distributed across green, fast-flag, typography, direct verbal behavior and tapestries. Globality is expressed through language/script/currency references rather than generic globe imagery.

## CRITIQUE / LIMITS
Ragged Edge reports conversion, product-adoption and share-price changes after launch. These are first-party correlation claims, not proof that the rebrand caused the results.

Training boundary: useful for studying how a finance brand can become highly expressive while maintaining operational clarity and a retained recognizer.
