# Samyang — Phase 1C Training Case

Status: VERIFIED TRAINING CASE — BOUNDED PARTIAL VISUAL PRIMARY

Learning role: Asian corporate transformation + naming/architecture + literal-strategy risk test.

Scope boundary: verified for international-growth context, Roundsquare naming, circle/square symbol, dual-mode typography, orange/charcoal palette and packaging/application-template scope. Complete Hangul system, full packaging rollout and independent effectiveness remain incomplete.

## BEFORE STATE
Samyang was already recognizable as a Korean instant-noodle manufacturer but wanted to increase international awareness and expand perception toward food technology and sustainability across a broader group of businesses.

## SOURCE-STATED PROBLEM / STRATEGY
Pentagram states that a new strategic approach centered on contradiction helped reposition the group and informed the new corporate name `Samyang Roundsquare Group`, unifying different business areas.

Primary source: https://www.pentagram.com/work/samyang

## OBSERVED SYSTEM
Primary case text and directly inspected visual carriers show:
- old hand-drawn/script-like Samyang expression replaced at corporate level by a geometric symbol and dark sans wordmark;
- orange circle and square physically overlap/interlock in the symbol;
- English and Korean lockups share the same corporate geometry;
- Fractul is used in a traditional and squarer version to create controlled typographic contradiction;
- charcoal + bright orange dominate, with supporting orange/burgundy range;
- iconography repeats curved/sharp opposition.

Visual carriers inspected:
- Korean corporate-identity coverage showing English/Korean lockups;
- Design Compass before/after carrier;
- Pentagram primary case-page image set and descriptions.

## PRESERVED / CHANGED
Preserved: Samyang name and orange lineage.
Changed: corporate naming/architecture, symbol, wordmark, type system and overall corporate tone.

## SYSTEM LOGIC
This case is valuable partly because it is dangerous: the circle/square concept maps the `Roundsquare` idea very literally. The system works when the contradiction is carried consistently into type and icons; it would become shallow if treated as proof that every strategic tension should be converted into one pictorial metaphor.

## CRITIQUE / LIMITS
Pentagram describes the result as ownable and robust, but no independent evidence was found that the circle/square device itself improves recognition or global awareness.

Training boundary: this is a positive example of disciplined literal translation with explicit limits. It should help the expert distinguish a system-generating metaphor from a one-off visual pun.
