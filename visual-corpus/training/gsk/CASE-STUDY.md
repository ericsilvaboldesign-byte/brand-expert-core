# GSK — Phase 1C Training Case

Status: VERIFIED TRAINING CASE — BOUNDED PARTIAL VISUAL PRIMARY

Learning role: corporate transformation + science-led identity + multi-environment brand system.

Scope boundary: verified for business-change context, strategy, signal/logo behavior at a bounded level, orange/gradient visual system, bioscience imagery, physical/digital dimensionality and the relationship between purpose and system language. Complete motion, full UI and independently verified effectiveness remain incomplete.

## BEFORE STATE
A demerger beginning in 2020 moved GSK away from consumer healthcare and renewed its focus on biopharma. The brand therefore faced a genuine organizational transition rather than a cosmetic refresh.

## SOURCE-STATED PROBLEM / STRATEGY
Wolff Olins states that it worked with GSK to bring a renewed purpose, strategy, voice and culture to life under `Get ahead of disease together`, across brand architecture, visual/verbal identity, employer brand, environments and digital experience.

Primary source: https://wolffolins.com/work/gsk

## OBSERVED SYSTEM
Existing primary repo evidence includes the 2022 GSK logo SVG. Primary agency text documents the dynamic `signal` and curved forms inspired by bioscience and the adaptable immune system. Direct visual carriers inspected show:
- the orange GSK form expanded into a modular spatial/3D language;
- orange-to-warm gradient behavior over white and neutral fields;
- bioscience/molecular imagery used as content rather than generic technology decoration;
- large curved containers and cutouts framing people/science imagery;
- dimensional physical/environmental expressions using the same curved geometry.

Visual carriers inspected:
- Creative Review: https://www.creativereview.co.uk/wolff-olins-gsk-identity/
- Creative Boom: https://www.creativeboom.com/inspiration/wolff-olins-gsk/
- existing primary SVG and manifest in `visual-corpus/training/gsk/`.

## PRESERVED / CHANGED
Preserved: GSK name and orange equity.
Changed: legacy lozenge-like expression becomes a forward-pointing signal and broader organic system; imagery becomes explicitly bioscientific; expression expands into spatial, digital and people-centered contexts.

## SYSTEM LOGIC
The strongest translation is not `science = futuristic graphics`; it is the repeated use of adaptive, curved forms and actual bioscience imagery across identity, environments and content. The logo is one manifestation of a larger behavior system.

## CRITIQUE / LIMITS
Wolff Olins reports 247,000 social mentions and 97% positive social sentiment after launch. These are first-party outcome claims and are not treated as causal proof that the visual identity drove business performance.

Training boundary: use this case to study how a corporate purpose and organizational reset can be translated into a broad expression system without reducing the brand to a literal scientific symbol.
