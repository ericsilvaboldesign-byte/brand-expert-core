# Burger King — Phase 1C Training Case

Status: VERIFIED TRAINING CASE — BOUNDED PARTIAL VISUAL PRIMARY

Learning role: heritage revival + QSR + packaging/physical brand + asset recombination.

Scope boundary: verified for the source-stated business/brand mismatch, heritage-led logo direction, warm food palette, packaging language, illustration/type behavior and physical/digital coherence. Full restaurant/environment rollout and independent causal effectiveness remain incomplete.

## BEFORE STATE
JKR frames the business as having already changed around food quality, sustainability and restaurant experience while the brand still looked like a glossy early-2000s system. The identity problem was therefore a mismatch between business direction and brand expression.

## SOURCE-STATED PROBLEM / STRATEGY
JKR's own case page positions the rebrand as a move toward a more real, food-led expression rather than a technology/gloss aesthetic.

Primary source: https://www.jkrglobal.com/work/burger-king

## OBSERVED SYSTEM
Visual carriers directly inspected show:
- return to a simplified bun/wordmark composition strongly related to Burger King's historical mark;
- warm red/orange/brown/cream palette with green accents;
- chunky, rounded typography designed to feel food-like rather than digital-tech neutral;
- packaging using large logo, large type and ingredient/food descriptors;
- playful illustration and ingredient graphics;
- mobile/digital communications using the same palette, type and illustration vocabulary;
- physical bags, cups and fries packaging that read as one system rather than isolated mockups.

Visual carriers:
- A Change of Brand Burger King case
- The One Show packaging archive
- JKR primary case-page images listed on the source page.

## PRESERVED / CHANGED
Preserved/revived: historical Burger King logo logic, food colors, irreverence and product centrality.
Changed: 2000s dimensional/gloss visual language, typography, packaging system, illustration and digital expression.

## SYSTEM LOGIC
The strategic move is not simply nostalgia. Historical codes are selectively reactivated because they better support the new food-first positioning. Typography, logo, packaging and color all reinforce appetite and recognizability, while digital surfaces inherit the same visual grammar.

## CRITIQUE / LIMITS
JKR reports stock and purchase-intent changes; award and industry sources report positive response. Treat all such numbers as first-party or campaign-period correlations unless independently causal evidence is available.

Training boundary: use this case to study when existing/heritage codes can outperform novelty—not as a rule that retro design is inherently more distinctive.
